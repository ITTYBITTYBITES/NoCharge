Web accessibility is built into the foundation of NoCharge: native semantic HTML elements (`<button>`, `<a>`, `<input>`), visible focus indicators, screen reader live regions, and full keyboard operability.

Because our games follow standard web specifications, they work seamlessly with specialized assistive hardware and adaptive peripherals.

## Adaptive input devices

**Ergonomic Trackballs.** For players with limited wrist mobility or tremor, a finger or thumb-operated trackball keeps the hand stationary while providing precise pointer movement for dragging in [Word Tile Rush](/games/word-tile-rush/) or selecting tiles in [Color Flip](/games/color-flip/).

**Adaptive Switches and Keypads.** Single-switch or multi-switch interfaces mapping to standard keyboard events (Space, Enter, Arrow keys) allow players with motor impairments to navigate and play titles like [Memory Match](/games/memory-match/) and [Beacon Lattice](/games/beacon-lattice/).

**High-Contrast & Large-Print Keyboards.** Yellow keycaps with bold black lettering assist players with low vision in finding keyboard shortcuts quickly without eye strain.

## Software synergy

NoCharge does not use closed canvas-only traps. Every button and interactive cell has accessible ARIA attributes, meaning OS-level switch access (such as Apple Switch Control or Android Switch Access) scans through game controls logically.

## Alternatives already nearby

Modern operating systems include powerful built-in accessibility software: VoiceOver (iOS/macOS), TalkBack (Android), Narrator/NVDA (Windows), and Sticky Keys. These tools are completely free and fully supported across all NoCharge routes.
