Monitor arms: clamp vs grommet is a practical question about screens and stands for quiet browser play. This guide explains observable tradeoffs without assuming a purchase is needed and without health, ergonomic, or productivity promises.

## Who this is for

Readers using NoCharge in a shared room, small desk, or varied lighting who want to understand screens and stands choices. It does not promise comfort, accessibility, or compatibility for every person.

## What to look for

Consider size, connection, material, adjustability, cleaning, cable routing, return policy, and whether the item fits the actual task. For screens and stands, check how it interacts with existing keyboard, pointing device, screen, and desk.

- Task fit: does it solve the specific friction you noticed?
- Space: does it fit the desk and leave room for the game board?
- Connection: wired, wireless, Bluetooth, and power needs.
- Maintenance: cleaning, battery, and storage.

## No-purchase checks

Test what you already own first: move the current screens and stands a few centimeters, adjust settings, clean contacts, try a different surface, or use keyboard alternatives where documented. Many frictions come from placement, not from needing a new item.

- Move the item away from hollow or reflective surfaces.
- Check operating-system settings for speed, gestures, or zoom.
- Clear clutter that forces awkward reach.
- Try an existing spare before shopping.
