Using comfortable headphones or earbuds helps you stay focused on logic puzzles without disturbing others in shared rooms or offices.

## Headphone styles compared for calm sessions

**Lightweight In-Ear Earbuds.** Minimal weight and zero headband pressure make wireless earbuds comfortable for quick breaks. Silicone tips provide passive isolation without the ear-clamping pressure of heavy over-ear models.

**Open-Back Over-Ear Headphones.** These allow airflow around your ears and create a natural soundstage. Because they do not seal your ears, they eliminate the pressure feeling during long solitaire sessions.

## What to prioritize

- **Low clamping force:** For puzzle play, prioritize lightweight frames (under 250 grams) that do not create jaw or crown tension.
- **Moderate volume:** Procedural audio tones do not require high volume. Keep audio at 30–50% to prevent ear fatigue.

## Alternatives already nearby

If you are alone in a quiet room, your phone or laptop's built-in speakers set to low volume provide clean feedback without wearing anything on your head.
