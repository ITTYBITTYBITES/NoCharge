Quiet Setup is for people who want to make browser play or a small desk routine simpler without turning that question into a shopping project. It covers controls, displays, stands, low-noise accessories, and paper puzzles. The starting assumption is that the keyboard, pointer, screen, or stack of books already nearby may be enough.

## The method

We begin with a task, not a product. For a keyboard, that task might be keeping arrow keys easy to find. For a stand, it might be holding a phone in landscape orientation while leaving its charging port clear. Criteria are chosen before any outbound link: function, space, adjustability, noise character, maintenance, and meaningful limitations.

Every launch guide is marked **Editorial research**. That means NoCharge compared broadly documented categories and design tradeoffs; it does not mean we personally used each item or conducted hands-on or laboratory testing. We do not copy merchant descriptions, ratings, prices, or customer reviews. A future article may carry a different evidence label only when there is real evidence to support it.

## Recommendations are optional paths

A recommendation should explain who it may suit, why it is relevant, and what could make it unsuitable. The guidance must still work if every paid link disappears. Amazon links, when present, are clearly marked and use a broad search page unless an owner-approved, specifically evaluated item is available. Commercial relationships do not determine NoCharge game design, scores, access, or progression.

There are useful no-purchase checks in nearly every topic:

- Try browser zoom before replacing a display.
- Move a keyboard or pointer before adding a desk accessory.
- Test both portrait and landscape with the stand or case you already have.
- Use a folded cloth under a noisy loose object before buying a mat.
- Borrow a puzzle format from a library before committing to a book.

## Boundaries and limitations

Quiet Setup does not provide medical, vision, accessibility, or professional ergonomic advice. A control method that works well for one reader can be difficult for another. We describe options and observable tradeoffs rather than promising comfort, productivity, health, cognition, or universal compatibility.

We also do not track prices, stock, discounts, ratings, or availability. Those facts change quickly and belong to the destination that provides them. The section is an editorial publication—not a store, product feed, or search tool.

## A practical next action

Choose one friction point in your current setup and write it as a neutral requirement: “I need visible arrow keys,” “the charging cable collides with the stand,” or “browser text is too small at the current zoom.” Try the free adjustment first. If it does not solve the problem, use the relevant [Quiet Setup index](/setup/) guide to compare categories rather than collecting a long shopping list.
