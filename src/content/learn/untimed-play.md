**Bottom line:** Untimed at NoCharge means no countdown or performance pressure — the game ends by its rules, not by a clock. 16 of 17 current games are untimed by default or offer an untimed mode; Word Tile Rush is the deliberate exception, and its guide says so plainly.

## What "untimed" does and does not mean

It means:

- No countdown, no per-move timer, no score penalty for thinking.
- The game can still be finished; it has a real end condition.
- Your session can be as long or as short as you want.

It does not mean:

- The game is easy (Nonogram and Beacon Lattice are untimed and can be hard).
- The game is short (a 9×9 Sudoku can take 20+ minutes).
- There are no mechanics at all (switches and sweeps still happen at your pace).

## How NoCharge hides pressure honestly

- **By default:** most titles load in an untimed mode.
- **By labeling:** the guide lists modes explicitly; the game card shows the session label; the Discovery Wheel pressure filter uses the same catalog facts.
- **By design:** no lives, energy, or "come back tomorrow" gates. The [daily hub](/daily/) streak is a local note, not a notification system.

## Games with untimed or both modes

16 of 17 games qualify today. Word Search, Nonogram, Mini Sudoku, Klondike, FreeCell, Memory Match, Beacon Lattice, Twenty Forty-Eight, Tile Garden, and the Pass & Play family are untimed. Color Flip offers both; Word Tile Rush is timed.

## Limits

Session labels are estimates. "Untimed" is a property of the mode, not an evaluation of difficulty, and NoCharge makes no cognitive or relaxation claims.
