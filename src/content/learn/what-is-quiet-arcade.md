**Bottom line:** Quiet Arcade is NoCharge's own term for a browser arcade built around calm defaults and honest documentation — untimed play where the genre allows, optional sound, local-only scores, no accounts, and no pressure mechanics like lives, energy, or timers designed to make you return. It is a description of how the catalog is made, not a promise about you.

## What it is

A Quiet Arcade game at NoCharge typically includes:

- **Calm defaults:** untimed as the default mode when the game's genre supports it; Word Tile Rush keeps its timed loop but the guide says so plainly.
- **Documented controls:** each game page lists pointer, touch, and keyboard paths, and the guide adds the full list with a comparison table.
- **Local results:** scores, wins, and progress live only in this browser under `nocharge:*` keys. There are no leaderboards.
- **Optional audio:** ambient textures and effects start off or low, mute is one control, and audio stops when the page is hidden.
- **No pressure loop:** no lives, energy refills, paywalls to continue, countdown-to-return, or streak-shaming. The [daily hub](/daily/) keeps streaks as a private note, not a notification system.
- **Explicit limits:** guides say what NoCharge did not evaluate and do not claim cognitive benefit.

## What it is not

Quiet Arcade is not:

- A genre label from anyone else. NoCharge invented the phrase for its own editorial voice.
- A guarantee of low stimulation. Color Flip in visual mode is still a reaction game; its turn-based mode is the calm alternative — an honest distinction, not a hidden one.
- A medical or wellness claim. NoCharge does not say these games reduce stress, improve focus, or train memory.

## How to spot a calm choice in the catalog

Use the [Discovery Wheel](/tools/discovery-wheel/) pressure filter for untimed games, or read the guide's mode section before playing. The registry facts page publishes the current count of untimed-or-both games and the date of the last catalog review.

## Limits

This definition describes the current catalog on 2026-08-27. It is a statement about NoCharge's product posture, not a standard other sites need to meet.
