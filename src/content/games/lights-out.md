Lights Out is a 5×5 toggle puzzle. Each press flips the pressed light and the lights directly above, below, left, and right. The goal is all lights off. Every puzzle is made by starting from an all-off board and pressing cells, so the inverse presses always solve it — a fact NoCharge states instead of hiding.
