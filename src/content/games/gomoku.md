Gomoku is the classic five-in-a-row game: two players take turns placing black and white stones on a 15×15 grid, and the first to make five or more stones in a row — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — wins. NoCharge implements the free-style variant: no overline restriction, no captures, no tournament opening rules, and no computer opponent.
