title: "My Arcade shows what this browser saved"
date: "2026-08-21"
summary: "A private local dashboard for recent play and each game's own saved result, with no account and no synchronization."
type: update
draft: false
---

My Arcade collects the game results already stored in the browser you are using: what you played recently, the best result or progress each game keeps, and a link back into the game and its guide. It is not an account, nothing is uploaded, and results do not move between browsers or devices. Clearing game data on Privacy — or from the matching control on the dashboard — removes the underlying values and leaves your separate analytics choice alone.
