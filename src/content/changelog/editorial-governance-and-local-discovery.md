NoCharge now documents its editorial standards and content-to-runtime review responsibilities. Four platform articles explain Quiet Arcade, local score storage, accessible game design, and the testing process without assigning those pages to a fake game.

Recently Played records only a stable game ID and timestamp after a meaningful game action, keeps at most four entries in this browser, and can be removed through Clear Game Data without changing analytics consent. Four reviewed collections state their inclusion methods and game-specific reasons; Word Tile Rush is intentionally absent from the untimed or reduced-pressure collection.

The accessibility checklist and Cloudflare owner handoff now cover the current four-game platform and clearly separate repository automation from manual or dashboard work. Game mechanics, AdSense IDs and placement, Google consent behavior, analytics consent, and Cloudflare configuration were not changed.
