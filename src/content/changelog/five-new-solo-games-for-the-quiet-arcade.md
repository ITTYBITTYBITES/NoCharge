Five more solo games are now available: [Klondike Solitaire](/games/klondike/) (draw-1 or draw-3, with keyboard card movement), [FreeCell](/games/freecell/) (all 52 cards face-up), [Nonogram](/games/nonogram/) (24 curated puzzles with a plain-text clue view), [2048](/games/twenty-forty-eight/), and [Tile Garden](/games/tile-garden/) (a calm merge game with three modes). Each one follows the Quiet Arcade principles: no timer where none is needed, no account, no leaderboard, no streak, and no dark patterns. Results stay in local storage as personal records, and a site-wide sound pass gives every game the same small, mutable set of result sounds.
