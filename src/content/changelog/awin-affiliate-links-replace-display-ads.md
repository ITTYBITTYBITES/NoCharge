The banner and the empty advertising areas are gone for good. In their place, some pages now end with an optional "recommended gear" panel, and a few Quiet Setup articles carry a paid link to an approved merchant in the Awin network.

Every paid link is a plain text link: no merchant images, no product widgets, no prices, and nothing loaded from a shop into the page. Links are marked "(paid link)", open in a new tab, and never appear inside a game board, game controls, or the Lab.

The Awin tracking script only loads if you allow the analytics-and-affiliate-tracking choice in the consent dialog. If you decline, nothing from Awin loads at all. Amazon links in Quiet Setup work as before.

See the [Advertising Disclosure](/advertising/) and [Privacy](/privacy/) pages for the full detail.
