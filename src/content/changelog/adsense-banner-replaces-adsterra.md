summary: "Removed the Adsterra banner and Smartlink integration and added a single manual, responsive Google AdSense banner with Google-managed consent."
type: privacy
draft: false
---

NoCharge removed the Adsterra banner and Smartlink integration entirely, including the sandboxed ad-host pages, the anti-adblock fallback, and the site's advertising consent toggle. The site now shows exactly one manual, responsive Google AdSense banner near the bottom of eligible pages, labeled "Advertisement", with Google's Privacy & messaging message as the advertising consent platform. Auto ads, interstitials, anchors, popups, and Smartlink behavior remain off, and ads never appear over gameplay.
