NoCharge is not currently serving display advertising. The banner near the bottom of eligible pages, the empty ad areas in the Lab, and the Google advertising consent tag are all switched off rather than left in place unfilled. Pages that used to show a banner simply end after their content.

The implementation has not been deleted. It sits behind a build flag so it can be restored without a redesign, and nothing about it loads while the flag is off.

Analytics works exactly as before: off until you allow it, changeable at any time. Affiliate links in Quiet Setup are unaffected. See the [Advertising Disclosure](/advertising/) for what is and is not commercial on the site today.
