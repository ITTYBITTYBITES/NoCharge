Pass &amp; Play is a new family of games for two people sharing one screen: Tic-Tac-Toe (3×3, 4×4, and first-to-3 matches), Dots &amp; Boxes (4×4 and 6×6), Four in a Row (7×6 and 6×5), Reversi (8×8 with legal-move hints and automatic passes), Last Token (misère take-away rounds), and Pass the Picture (a cooperative shared canvas with a local PNG download). Every turn passes through one shared handoff screen that names the next player and keeps the match tally. Player names last only for the browser session and are never stored; each game keeps a single most-recent match record on the device, shown in the new Pass &amp; Play section of My Arcade and removed by the same Clear game data control. There are no accounts, no timers, no computer opponents, and no network play anywhere in the set.
