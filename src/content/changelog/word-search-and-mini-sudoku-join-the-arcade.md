Two more solo games are now available: [Word Search](/games/word-search/) (eight curated themes, 8×8 and 10×10 grids, hints that identify a starting letter) and [Mini Sudoku](/games/mini-sudoku/) (a 6×6 grid with 3×2 boxes, pencil marks, an on-screen digit pad, and a unique-solution generator). Both are untimed and keep only a completion count in local storage; each ships with a field guide and a game article.
