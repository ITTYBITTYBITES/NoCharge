## Controls at a glance

Select the first letter and the last letter of a straight word. Mouse and touch players tap cells directly; keyboard players move a focus cursor with the arrow keys and press Enter or Space to select. A **Show word list** button keeps the remaining words visible, **Hint** highlights one starting cell and names its letter, and **New puzzle** starts a fresh grid (asking first if words are already found).

## How selection works

Words may run horizontally, vertically, or diagonally, forwards or backwards. A found word stays highlighted. Hint identifies a first letter only.

## Accessibility

The grid uses labeled buttons, live announcements, visible focus, and a layout that can scroll on a narrow screen. Reduced motion changes animation only; it does not change sound preferences.
