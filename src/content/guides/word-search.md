## Controls at a glance

Tap the first letter and the last letter of a straight word. Keyboard players can focus grid cells and use the same direct selection. A **Show word list** button keeps the remaining words visible.

## How selection works

Words may run horizontally, vertically, or diagonally, forwards or backwards. A found word stays highlighted. Hint identifies a first letter only.

## Accessibility

The grid uses labeled buttons, live announcements, visible focus, and a layout that can scroll on a narrow screen. Reduced motion changes animation only; it does not change sound preferences.

## What we do not claim

This is a quiet word-finding game. We do not claim an optimal theme for vocabulary building or any educational outcome.
