## Controls at a glance

Select a cell — tap it, move the focus cursor with the arrow keys, or Tab into the grid — then type 1–6 or press a number on the on-screen digit pad. Backspace or the ✕ pad button clears the cell, U undoes, C checks, and R reveals the selected cell. The **Marks** button toggles pencil notes.

## Why 6×6

Six rows and columns with 3×2 boxes make a smaller alternative to 9×9. Difficulty is the documented cell count: Easy removes 12, Medium 16, and Hard 20.

## Pencil marks

Turn on the **Marks** button, select an empty cell, and type digits to add or remove notes; ✕ or Backspace clears them. Notes help you hold possibilities while you inspect a row, column, and box. This is a helpful option, not a claim of an optimal method.

## Accessibility and limits

Visible focus, live updates, high-contrast-friendly borders, and a keyboard path support different play styles. We do not claim brain training, cognitive benefit, or a best solving approach.
