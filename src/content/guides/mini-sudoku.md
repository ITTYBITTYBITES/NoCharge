## Controls at a glance

Select a cell and type 1–6. Arrow keys move the selection, Backspace clears, U undoes, C checks, and R reveals the selected cell.

## Why 6×6

Six rows and columns with 3×2 boxes make a smaller alternative to 9×9. Difficulty is the documented cell count: Easy removes 12, Medium 16, and Hard 20.

## Pencil marks

Use candidate notes to hold possibilities while you inspect a row, column, and box. This is a helpful option, not a claim of an optimal method.

## Accessibility and limits

Visible focus, live updates, high-contrast-friendly borders, and a keyboard path support different play styles. We do not claim brain training, cognitive benefit, or a best solving approach.
