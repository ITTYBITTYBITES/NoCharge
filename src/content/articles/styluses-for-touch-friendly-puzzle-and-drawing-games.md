While fingers work well for tapping large cards in [Klondike](/games/klondike/), games with drawing inputs or detailed grids—such as [Pass the Picture](/games/pass-the-picture/), [Word Search](/games/word-search/), and [Nonogram](/games/nonogram/)—benefit significantly from the precision of a stylus.

A stylus prevents your finger from blocking the line you are drawing and provides a natural pen-and-paper feel.

## Stylus types compared

**Passive Conductive Mesh / Rubber Styluses.** These work on any capacitive touchscreen without batteries, pairing, or charging. Micro-knit conductive fiber tips glide smoothly across glass without sticking.

**Precision Disc Styluses.** These feature a clear plastic disc around a fine ballpoint tip, allowing you to see exactly where your line begins while maintaining electrical contact with the screen.

**Active Bluetooth Pens (Apple Pencil, S Pen, USI).** Supported by specific tablet models, active styluses offer sub-pixel precision, palm rejection, and zero lag. While great for Pass the Picture, they are not necessary for casual grid games.

## Stylus vs. finger tradeoffs

| Factor | Finger Tap | Passive Mesh Stylus | Precision Disc |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Drawing line visibility** | Often obscured by finger | Visible around tip | Completely visible through disc |
| **Setup required** | None | None | None |
| **Battery / charging** | Never | Never | Never |
| **Screen smudges** | Leaves oils and prints | Keeps glass clean | Keeps glass clean |
