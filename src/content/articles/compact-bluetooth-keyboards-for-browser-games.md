Many browser games on NoCharge offer full keyboard support. Titles like [Memory Match](/games/memory-match/) (arrow keys and Space), [Beacon Lattice](/games/beacon-lattice/) (number keys and grid navigation), and [Twenty Forty-Eight](/games/twenty-forty-eight/) (arrow keys) can be played faster and more comfortably with dedicated physical keys than with touch taps.

When pairing an external keyboard with a tablet or mobile phone, a compact form factor saves desk space and makes quick play sessions seamless.

## Form factor comparisons for casual play

**60% Keyboards.** These omit dedicated arrow keys, function rows, and the number pad. While extremely compact, they require holding a function modifier (`Fn`) to access arrow keys, which is awkward for directional games.

**65% Keyboards.** A popular sweet spot: they retain dedicated arrow keys and navigation keys in a tight footprint only one column wider than a 60% board. This layout works out of the box with NoCharge's keyboard-friendly catalog.

**Scissor-Switch Membranes.** Ultra-slim keyboards modeled after laptop layouts offer quiet keystrokes and light actuation, making them ideal for quiet spaces and casual puzzle play.

## Key considerations

- **Multi-device pairing:** Look for keyboards that switch between 2–3 Bluetooth devices with a single shortcut (e.g., swapping from a work laptop to an iPad).
- **Wake-from-sleep latency:** Some low-power Bluetooth keyboards take 1–2 seconds to reconnect after being idle. For puzzle games with long thinking pauses, choose models with fast reconnect times.
- **Battery life:** Rechargeable USB-C lithium batteries last weeks between charges, while AAA-powered models can run for months before battery swaps.
