**Bottom line:** NoCharge games open instantly without an account, subscription, email, or download. Scores and progress stay in this browser's localStorage under `nocharge:*` keys, analytics load only if you allow them, and one labeled ad banner sits before the footer — never over the game. That is the complete free model.

## What you get with zero signup

- Every game, tool, collection, and daily, immediately playable.
- Local best scores, win counts, puzzle progress, and one bounded Pass & Play match record per game.
- Keyboard paths where the game supports them, plus optional sound and ambient.
- A privacy page that lists every storage key and a single "Clear game data" control.

## What "free" does not mean

- **No cloud save:** storage is per browser, per device. Clearing site data removes it.
- **No "unblocked" promise:** whether the site loads on a school network depends on that network. [Read the honest distinction](/learn/unblocked-vs-no-account/).
- **No offline install:** after first load, HTTP cache may replay assets, but NoCharge does not ship a service worker.
- **No analytics by default:** the consent model keeps GA blocked until you allow it.

## Limits

The free model is the privacy model: because there is no account, there is no recovery if you clear data, no restore from another device, and no support channel for "sync my progress". NoCharge states these as limits rather than masking them with a login wall "for your convenience".
