Modern smartphones are wide, heavy, and covered in slippery glass. When holding a phone for multiple rounds of [Color Flip](/games/color-flip/) or [Word Tile Rush](/games/word-tile-rush/), most people support the bottom edge with their pinky finger—a posture known to cause strain and tendon fatigue over time.

An ergonomic grip transfers the phone's weight to the center of your palm, allowing your fingers to tap and swipe relaxed.

## Grip styles compared

**Collapsible Socket Grips.** These expand outward when in use and collapse flat to slip into pockets. They provide a secure two-finger hold and double as a horizontal table kickstand for resting your phone during play.

**Magnetic Ring Holders.** Compatible with MagSafe and magnetic cases, ring grips rotate 360 degrees to accommodate any hand position and can be removed instantly for wireless charging.

**Elastic Silicone Finger Straps.** A soft, flat strap that lies flush against the phone case. You slide one or two fingers beneath the strap for a secure grip with zero added pocket thickness.

## Ergonomic tips for mobile play

1. **Alternate hands:** Switch between your dominant and non-dominant hand every few rounds of solitaire or word hunting.
2. **Rest your elbows:** Avoid holding the phone in mid-air with bent wrists; resting your forearms on a table or lap cushion relieves shoulder tension.
3. **Use two-handed grip for wide boards:** Games like [FreeCell](/games/freecell/) benefit from two-thumb operation rather than stretching one thumb across all 8 columns.

## Table-resting alternative

Placing your phone on a small silicone coaster or mousepad keeps it from sliding around on a table, allowing comfortable index-finger tapping without gripping the phone at all.
