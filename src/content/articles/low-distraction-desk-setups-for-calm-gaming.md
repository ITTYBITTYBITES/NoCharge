The core philosophy of NoCharge is *Quiet Arcade*—games without flash ads, loud popups, subscription nags, or countdown pressure. But your physical environment plays an equal role in how calm and focused a puzzle session feels.

Visual clutter in your peripheral vision, notifications buzzing on your phone, and messy desks elevate background stress. Here are practical ways to organize a low-distraction desk space.

## Physical desk organization

**Clear the immediate 18-inch radius.** Keep your primary work area free of stray mail, mugs, and loose cords. Having only your input device (mouse, keyboard, or tablet) in front of you helps your mind settle into the logic puzzle at hand.

**Cable management.** Fastening cables beneath your desk or using simple velcro ties removes visual tangle from your line of sight.

**Warm, diffuse lighting.** Harsh overhead fluorescent lighting can cause subtle tension. A small desk lamp with a warm 2700K–3000K bulb creates an inviting, cozy atmosphere.

## Digital distraction reduction

1. **Enable Focus or Do Not Disturb:** Mute incoming chat and email notifications while playing games like [Tile Garden](/games/tile-garden/) or [Nonogram](/games/nonogram/).
2. **Close background tabs:** Running dozens of open tabs with flashing notification badges disrupts your concentration.
3. **Use Focus mode in NoCharge:** The built-in Focus mode expands the game board and dims surrounding interface elements.
