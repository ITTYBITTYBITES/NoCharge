**Bottom line:** NoCharge runs 16 of 17 games untimed by default or with an untimed mode. "Untimed" means no countdown; it does not mean easy or short. The [untimed collection](/collections/untimed-or-reduced-pressure-browser-games/) and the [Discovery Wheel](/tools/discovery-wheel/) pressure filter both read the same catalog facts.

## What untimed changes

- You can pause to reason; a wrong guess costs a move, not a second.
- The game ends by its rules — all pairs found, puzzle solved, board cleared — not by a clock.
- Elapsed time, where recorded at all, is a post-win metric (Minesweeper) or simply absent.

## Games that are untimed

Nonogram, Beacon Lattice, Mini Sudoku 6×6, Sudoku 9×9, Word Search, Memory Match, Klondike, FreeCell, Twenty Forty-Eight, Tile Garden, Minesweeper, Hangman, Lights Out, Simon, and every Pass & Play title. Color Flip offers both visual (timed) and turn-based (untimed) modes. Word Tile Rush is the timed exception, stated plainly.

## Honest caveats

- **Untimed ≠ trivial:** Nonogram and Beacon Lattice can be hard; Sudoku 9×9 runs 8–25 minutes at the top of its label.
- **Untimed ≠ endless:** every puzzle has an end condition.
- **Session labels are estimates:** the catalog reports ranges, not guarantees.

## How to verify

Open any game, check the guide's mode section, then use the [Discovery Wheel](/tools/discovery-wheel/) with the pressure filter set to untimed. The [registry facts page](/articles/registry-facts/) publishes the exact current percentage of untimed-or-both games and the review date.
