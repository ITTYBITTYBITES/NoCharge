A focused shelf of NoCharge word games. The category is defined by what the player does — letters, words, spelling — not by a scoring model: one is timed, two are untimed, and all three state their mechanics honestly.

## Why this collection exists

Word games often arrive as clones of a famous daily word. NoCharge keeps its word shelf distinct: an original timed chain puzzle (Word Tile Rush), a classic untimed hunt (Word Search), and themed Hangman with accessible letter entry. This collection explains each one's core loop so you can choose the pace you want.

## How we decide inclusion

1. The core loop uses letters, words, or spelling.
2. Controls are documented on the game page and in the guide.
3. Results stay in this browser (`nocharge:*` keys documented on Privacy).

More word titles — anagrams, a Boggle-style grid with timed and untimed modes, and a category guesser — will join this collection as they ship, with their own rules and guides.

## What you will find in the grid

Each card shows genre, session length, and a reason tied to its specific rules. Session labels are estimates; the guides give the exact end conditions (grid full, word list complete, sixth miss).

## Limits

- Word Tile Rush is timed by design; the other two are untimed. This collection does not claim one pace is better.
- No word list is a vocabulary curriculum, and NoCharge makes no spelling or vocabulary-learning claims.
- Local results are per browser and per device; nothing syncs.
