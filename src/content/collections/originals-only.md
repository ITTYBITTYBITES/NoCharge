These are the games NoCharge designed rather than reimplemented. They are not necessarily “better” than the classics — the classification says where the rules came from, not how good they are.

## Why this collection exists

NoCharge ships both classic rules (Klondike, Nonogram, Sudoku) and originals. This collection separates the two so readers can find the custom designs without assuming the classics are clones of a specific brand — and without assuming originals are superior.

## How we decide inclusion

1. The ruleset is not a named third-party game: no clone of Wordle, Spelling Bee, 2048, or any trademarked title by name or trade dress.
2. The mechanics are implemented first for NoCharge, with original naming and art.
3. The catalog's `isOriginal` field is true and the classification is reviewed with each catalog change.

Twenty Forty-Eight is excluded: it is NoCharge's original implementation of a well-known 2048-style merge, but the rules are not NoCharge's.

## What you will find in the grid

Each card shows genre, session length, and the rule-set fact that qualifies it. Originals also carry a stronger guide and at least one systems article explaining how the mechanic differs from familiar games — see Beacon Lattice's exact-coverage article and Color Flip's visual-versus-turn-based article.

## Limits

- Original does not mean free of influence; mechanics can be inspired by familiar puzzles.
- Original does not mean tested-with-you or medically beneficial.
- NoCharge does not claim novelty protection; this is a factual editorial classification.
