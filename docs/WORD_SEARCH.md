# Word Search

Word Search is an untimed 8×8 or 10×10 single-player game. Twelve words from one of eight curated general-audience themes are placed in eight straight directions. Select first and last cells; found words remain highlighted. Hint reveals a first letter only.

The pure generator validates placements and falls back safely for malformed lists. Keyboard and touch use labeled cells, live found-word announcements, visible word lists, focus, forced-colors-friendly borders, zoom, and narrow-screen scrolling. Solved count and last theme are local keys: `nocharge:word-search:puzzles-solved` and `nocharge:word-search:last-list`.

There is no timer, score, streak, leaderboard, or educational claim. We do not claim an optimal vocabulary-building theme or outcome.
