# Shattered Foil local economy

Stars and shards are local progression markers with no real-money value and no transfer mechanism.

- Journey completion awards 1–3 stars according to the deal objective and 10 shards per awarded star.
- A first daily completion awards one star and 20 shards; replaying the same UTC date does not grant another canonical reward.
- A normal first win awards 10 shards.
- Card backs have fixed shard prices listed in the Atelier. Classic Copper Foil and Notre-Dame Azure begin owned.
- Purchases and currency deductions occur in one IndexedDB transaction, duplicate purchases do not charge again, and insufficient balances leave both records unchanged.
- Atmospheres are accessibility-safe presentation choices and begin unlocked; they are not sold.
