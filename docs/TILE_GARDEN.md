# Tile Garden

## Rules

8×8 grid. 4 tiers: seed → sprout → bloom → flower. Place seed tiles on empty cells. 4 same-tier same-species tiles in a 2×2 block auto-merge into one tier-up tile.

6 species × 4 tiers = 24 possible tile types.

## Modes

- **Garden (default):** Standard rules; "win" when a flower appears near center.
- **Meadow (endless):** No win condition.
- **Sketch (creative):** No auto-merges; free placement and removal.

## My Arcade Keys

| Key | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| `nocharge:tile-garden:best-tier` | Highest tier tile created (0-3) |

## Calm Design Constraints

- No lives
- No timer
- No streak
- No daily challenge
- No energy/buy-moves
- No leaderboard

## Art Rationale

Original plant emoji (🌱🌿🌼🌸) with species indicators. No copyrighted art. Tier distinction via background color gradient.
