NoCharge · Replacement Batch 01 · R3

Individual previews · supplemental category icons

Ten purpose-built SVG proposals for labels/sections that have no reusable compact icon source. The live logo, favicon set, social image, per-game covers, and existing guide glyphs are retained unchanged.

Review only. Every item is pending explicit review; none is approved or integrated. SVG canvases are transparent. Preview panels use the live site’s charcoal background and do not form part of the artwork.
