# NoCharge · Batch 01 replacement R3 · proposal

**Status:** 10 proposals awaiting explicit review · not approved · not integrated.\
**Direction:** supplemental category symbols drawn from NoCharge's live game and tool taxonomy—not a rebrand.

## Why these ten, and why no logo/art replacements

The live site already has a distinctive inline NoCharge mark + editable wordmark, a complete family of favicon/PWA files, a 1200 × 630 share image, and 26 functioning 800 × 800 game-cover artworks. Those existing assets are retained; R3 does not reproduce them.

A narrower gap remains: the live site has game genres, the Pass & Play collection, Daily, Guides, and Tools, but no matching **reusable, compact source icons** for those labels. Its 800 px cover pictures are individual game scenes, not readable 18–24 px category marks. The guide index's per-game emoji markers are also left unchanged. The icons below are supplemental labels for optional future use beside the existing text, not replacements for covers, the logo, search, or guide-card glyphs.

## Consistency rules

- **Use only the live site language:** transparent line icons, `viewBox="0 0 24 24"`, 2.2-unit rounded caps/joins, the site's muted `#9a9a9a` and strong green `#12b66a`.
- Keep the artwork background transparent. The existing `#121212` canvas and `#1c1c1c` panel should show through. No tiles behind icons, pastel blocks, glows, gradients, drop shadows, patterns, or decorative ornaments.
- Use at about 18–24 px next to an existing readable text label, matching the live 18 px search-icon scale. On mobile, scale SVGs down without cropping or replacing labels.
- Keep each subject literal to the existing game/tool category. No letters/numbers, logo shapes, new wordmark, fictional games, screenshots, extra UI, or generated text appear inside the artwork.
- All assets are hand-authored SVG; no image generation or external font is used. Keep the editable source and losslessly minified copy separate.
