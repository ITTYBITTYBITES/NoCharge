## Technical and visual notes

- **Major technical issue:** all ten generated PNGs are RGB images with no alpha channel. Although transparency was requested for several, the generator rendered a checkerboard into the pixels. It is visible in the individual files and on the contact sheet. The checkerboard has not been removed or disguised. These files must not be used for transparent production placements as-is.
- WebP derivatives preserve the source dimensions and reduce each file to roughly 25–72 KB from roughly 0.85–1.35 MB. They remain opaque and retain the checkerboard issue. They are review/optimization derivatives only.
- Visual review points: NC02 may read as a strong N-like mark; NC03's pattern is very fine and sparse; NC08's six-ray form may read as a pinwheel. These are decisions for review, not automatic revision instructions.
- The set uses a related orbit/path/rounded-shape language and the existing NoCharge palette. No copyrighted characters, external logos, fake interface screenshots, or embedded copy were requested or added.
