NoCharge · Batch 01 · R3.1 color adaptation

Ten category marks, ready for light and dark

The R3 visual direction is retained. These versioned proposals change only the icon color semantics: neutral strokes inherit the surrounding text color, green accents use a theme-provided CSS variable, and the canvas has no background.

Review only. R3 files remain untouched as history. R3.1 is not approved or integrated. A light-mode accent of #0f7d47 and dark-mode accent of #12b66a both clear 3:1 non-text contrast on their intended surfaces; every symbol remains paired with readable text.
