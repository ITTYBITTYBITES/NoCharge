NoCharge · Replacement Batch 01 · R2

Individual asset previews

Ten distinct, manually authored vector proposals based on the existing NoCharge V2 site. The design premise is modular wayfinding—not illustration-led artwork.

Review status: all proposals are awaiting your review, not approved, and not integrated. R1 has been rejected as a visual direction and remains archived separately. Backgrounds are transparent where appropriate; NC04 has an edge-to-edge lime tile with transparent rounded corners and NC10 intentionally uses an opaque social-preview background.
